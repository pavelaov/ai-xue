About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Cyberspace Security at the College of Computer Science and Technology, National University of Defense Technology (NUDT), with graduation expected in 2027.

My research focuses on AI security, privacy-preserving machine learning (PPML), confidential computing, and cloud computing security.

Available for an internship of six months or longer. I am exploring opportunities in AI, privacy computing, and research and development.
